In a release meeting, “Are the blockers cleared?” can be followed by a confident “Yeah,” then a customer promise. The conversation sounds aligned. The issue tracker may disagree.

ClashPoint checks the gap between a meeting commitment and its supporting records. This edition builds on my earlier ClashPoint prototype; inherited interface, rules and fixtures are disclosed in PROVENANCE.md rather than represented as new work.

## The meaningful Decider decision

Every final turn goes to Decider 2B with recent dialogue and two typed questions: which verification tool applies, and what intent the utterance establishes. Options include approval, dependency, capacity, ownership, prior decision, ignore and clarify. The returned choice controls the next tool, not a decorative badge.

Both questions share one state and one inference request. Low confidence asks for clarification. Social talk and reversals avoid retrieval/generation. A tentative proposal cannot become an interrupt-level finding.

## What Bedrock does

Bedrock Converse interprets unfamiliar phrasing when the deterministic resolver abstains. After a tool verifies a conflict, Bedrock explains the consequence and proposes conditional wording. Quotations come directly from server-owned evidence; returned evidence IDs are validated before display.

Numeric capacity, permissions and source status remain code checks. Provider failures are named explicitly, never translated into an all-clear.

## Demonstration

A context-dependent confirmation contradicts an open dependency; a promise encounters a pending approval; another P0 assignment exceeds capacity. Every finding exposes exact evidence and revision information. A clearly labeled simulated approval update retires the obsolete warning while preserving its earlier evidence snapshot.

GitHub and Notion records are fixtures. Offline rehearsal invokes neither model. The qualifying video must show actual live AWS inference.

## Limits

One tool family per utterance. Unknown entities, compound actions and unsupported paraphrases may request clarification. Confidence is not a safety guarantee, the threshold needs evaluation and hosting is not free. This is a read-only single-principal experiment.
